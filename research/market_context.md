### Tech / AI (QQQ, NVDA, TSM, AVGO)
- The dominant secular theme — unaffected by Iran war outcome

- Broadcom: multi-gigawatt deal with Anthropic (among others). ASIC pipeline expanding.
- TSMC: 72% foundry market share, ~24x forward P/E = most value in the AI stack

### Consumer Discretionary (XLY)
- Lower oil prices = lower gasoline = more consumer spending power
- Inflation easing → real wages rise → discretionary purchases recover
- XLY holds AMZN (largest weight), TSLA, Home Depot, Nike — all recovery beneficiaries
- Currently at $119.85; fair value closer to $135 in a soft landing / peace scenario

### Airlines (JETS)
- Most leveraged pure play on peace deal
- Two tailwinds: (1) fuel costs drop as oil normalizes, (2) Middle East/Asian airspace reopens
- JETS crushed during war (high fuel costs, route disruptions); mean reversion trade
- Current: $27.61. Pre-war levels were ~$32–35. Recovery target: $31–33 (+12–19%)

### Small Caps (IWM)
- Most leveraged to Fed rate cut expectations
- Lower inflation → Fed can cut → small caps re-rate significantly
- IWM at $282; recovery target $310–320 if rate cuts materialize in H2 2026
- Higher risk — skip if macro uncertainty remains

### Defense (ITA) — REMOVED FROM PORTFOLIO

### Energy (XLE) — SIGNIFICANTLY REDUCED

# Market Context — May 7, 2026

## Macro Environment

### Iran War / Energy Crisis
- US-Iran conflict began early 2026; Strait of Hormuz effectively closed to tanker traffic
- Largest oil supply disruption in history: ~12M bpd offline (more than 1970s + Ukraine combined)
- Brent crude: $60.85 (Jan 1) → $118.35 peak (end of March) → ~$111 current
- Demand expected to contract 80kb/d in 2026; IEA considering strategic reserve release
- **Risk:** Oil prices staying elevated → inflation → recession (CNBC: "sleepwalking into recession")
- **Opportunity:** Energy stocks/ETFs still have fundamental support at $100+ oil

### S&P 500 / Equities
- S&P 500 at all-time highs (~7,315); SPY at $731.53
- YTD return ~+4.5% (Q1 dropped 4.4%, has since recovered)
- Analyst consensus year-end 2026 target: 7,500–8,100 (+3–11% from current)
- Q1 earnings: 7 sectors reporting double-digit growth led by Comm Services, IT, Consumer Disc
- **Risk:** Stretched valuations, margin for error thin, oil shock could derail earnings

### AI Infrastructure
- AI spending accelerating; hyperscalers (AWS, Azure, GCP) expanding capacity aggressively
- Broadcom (AVGO): Major multi-gigawatt deal with Anthropic; strong ASIC pipeline
- TSMC: Controls 72% of foundry market; ~24x forward P/E (cheaper than NVDA/AVGO on earnings basis)
- Micron (MU): HBM3E demand at record levels; key GPU memory supplier
- Marvell (MRVL): Emerging inside hyperscaler data centers
- **Opportunity:** AI capex cycle is multi-year; corrections are buying opportunities

### Defense
- Iran war driving elevated global defense budgets
- ITA (iShares US Aerospace & Defense) at $222.69
- Multi-year cycle: NATO members increasing spending, US defense appropriations up

## Risks to Watch

1. **Oil shock → recession:** If $110+ oil persists, consumer spending weakens and S&P earnings miss
2. **Iran escalation:** Any widening of conflict could spike oil further and crater equities
3. **AI capex pause:** If hyperscalers cut spending, semiconductors get hit hard
4. **Fed policy:** Elevated oil = elevated inflation = less room for rate cuts
5. **Valuation compression:** S&P at 26.5x P/E — expensive if earnings disappoint

## Catalysts to Watch

1. **Peace talks / Hormuz reopening:** Would crash oil, boost consumer/discretionary
2. **Earnings beats in AI:** NVDA, AVGO, MU earnings drive tech higher
3. **Fed cut:** Any rate cut or cut signal is bullish for equities
4. **Demand destruction in oil:** If recession fears grow, oil could reverse sharply

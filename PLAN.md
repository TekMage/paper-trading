## Risk Management Rules

### Position limits
- No single position > 20% of portfolio
- No single sector > 35% of portfolio (energy currently at ~25% max)
- Wheel assignments can push a position up; cap covered call positions at 25%

### Stop losses
- Individual ETF positions: Stop review (not auto-stop) if down 15% from entry
- Wheel puts: Roll if premium doubles (i.e., put goes 50% in the money)
- Portfolio-level: If account falls below $87,500 (-12.5%), pause all new positions and reassess

### Profit taking
- Opportunistic Layer 3 positions: Take profits at +20%
- Layer 1/2: Let run; only adjust on major macro change

### Weekly review checklist
- [ ] Account value vs SPY benchmark
- [ ] Review any options positions approaching expiry
- [ ] Roll or close positions as needed
- [ ] Assess macro environment (oil prices, earnings, Fed)
- [ ] Consider new Layer 3 trade if cash available

## Options Reference

### Implied Volatility Environment (May 2026)
Higher IV due to Iran war uncertainty = **richer premiums**. This is favorable for option sellers.
Sell premium when IV is elevated, buy it back when IV compresses.

### Key expiration dates to use
- **Weekly (0–7 DTE):** Layer 3 opportunistic options only. Gamma risk is high.
- **Monthly (21–45 DTE):** Primary timeframe for Wheel and covered calls. Best theta decay curve.
- **Quarterly (60–90 DTE):** Only for deep ITM covered calls on large assigned positions.

### Greeks targets
- Delta on CSPs: 0.20–0.30 (sells at ~20–30% probability of assignment)
- Theta: Maximize — favor 30–45 DTE at open
- Vega: Sell when IV rank > 40%
